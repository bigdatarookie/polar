## Proposed exact utility/statistic contract

For each complete eligible cluster and each leg, W0 is portfolio wealth at the entry reference close immediately BEFORE its current-close fills/fees, with then-current holdings and cash marked at that reference price. W1 is post-fill/post-fee NAV at the cluster label-end close. Return=(W1/W0)-1, positive loss=max(0,-return). This includes entry costs in the event loss; all later re-entry/cost exposure remains in whole-path and burden metrics. If required pre/post ledger witnesses cannot be obtained without an unsupported inversion, return a concrete model gap before finance; do not silently use post-fee W0. W0<=0 or a nonpositive/default NAV path preserves default/deficit and invalid ordinary return/growth metrics rather than clipping it to zero or claiming a regular successful sample.

All clusters enter the common loss vector, INCLUDING zero-loss observations. Let N be cluster count and k=max(1,ceil(N/20)). Empirical ES95 is the mean of exactly the k largest nonnegative losses, ordered by loss then fixed cluster ID for ties. Equal boundary losses have identical value; tie identity is recorded but no additional tied observations are included to change the mean. N=0 =>UNAVAILABLE_NO_COMPLETE_CLUSTERS. FULL_VTI ES95=0 =>relative protection criterion UNAVAILABLE_BENCHMARK_ES_ZERO, not automatically MET. This is a finite descriptive order statistic, not a population95percent risk estimate or calibrated forecast. N/k and worst cases remain in the private assessment; do not infer independent evidence from documents/minutes, event frequency or native accounts. No bootstrap, p-value or population confidence is proposed.

The six proposed criteria apply to BOTH FINAL cost scenarios independently; all must be MET for a conditional point-screen preference, otherwise preserve NOT_MET/UNAVAILABLE and its exact reason. VALIDATION scenarios are diagnostic and carry no confirmatory preference decision. No choosing the better cost path.
