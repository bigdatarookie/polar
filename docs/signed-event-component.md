# Supplied signed event component

This PRIVATE component advances one supplied paired account/path on the accepted signed core/native bridge. It does not select scores, policy, shocks or source data, construct a 48-account study, admit a scientific attempt, publish common results, or operate a broker. Original historical source clocks may remain null; modeled events and locate scenarios do not establish PIT availability, legal entitlement, inventory or funding rights.

## Callable boundary

`polar.signed_event_driver`: frozen AccountContext, Rate, Action, Locate, Event and OrderRequest; SignedEventDriver(context, bridge). Construct the existing SignedNativeBridge with continue_economic_events=True. The default False preserves earlier component halt behavior. Expose the driver as the only order gateway in the hosting Strategy.

Call begin_event(Event), then next_order() at finite successive native routing boundaries. A returned OrderRequest must be bound exactly to one native market order and passed to submit(order). Feed real native callbacks to on_fill(fill) and on_terminal(order_id,status); do not mark requested quantities filled. A pending command/partial order bars another submission. When next_order() returns None the event is finalized. packet() returns the completed event evidence; failure_evidence(error) retains an active technical prefix and actual bridge errors and is deliberately rejected as completed evidence. The host must save this prefix and its physical observations before disposing a failed native account. No technical halt is cleared.

Events are supplied in strict UTC order, coalescing same-time source actions in one Event. TARGET/EXIT quantities are frozen declarations from an earlier decision (decision_ns plus latency no later than execution); this component does not calculate original D targets or rank assets. D-book receipt-to-target generation remains the later fixed-rule producer. Context/formation/source IDs and the complete declarations persist in the packet. Supplied marks bind identified MarkEvents; execution prices equal those supplied reference marks. No executable prices at EX/OBSERVATION. EX midnight cannot invent a new price relative to the preceding event. Missing executable prices preserve the actual position, claims and pending flatten.

## Ordered effects

Previous-event positions/marks/free cash/rates determine elapsed integer civil-second accrual on ACT/365. Recognition uses cumulative exact rational stream totals and settled origins. Supported rates are per-name BORROW, DEBIT_INTEREST and FREE_CASH_INTEREST. No unstated rebate base is inferred. At modeled UTC ex-midnight, cash-distribution claims use actual pre-event signed holdings; currency/date/identity or unsupported actions are explicitly retained and block dependent entry. Ex recognition is not payment. Stale cum-mark/new-claim provenance, mark age, NAV and default remain visible.

Identified current marks precede maintenance topup, separate equity requirement and buffer. Per-name maintenance is ceiling((1+short coefficient)*marked short cents), so baseline130% and supplied stress160% differ from initial150% collateral. Topup moves actual positive free cash only and is NAV-neutral. A newly observed breach/recall queues full flatten strictly after that observation plus latency; it cannot trade at its own observed CLOSE. A previously eligible flatten or predeclared EXIT performs cover pass, lexical long-sale pass, then exactly one retry of blocked covers. Same-event sale proceeds are a research settlement convention, not broker credit.

The CLOSE checkpoint follows eligible liquidation and precedes ordinary instructions: distributions ordered by payment date/name/action, then accrued components by stable stream ID. NO_CREDIT failures preserve unpaid liabilities and MODEL_OVERDUE_PAYMENT_EPISODE history, distinct from NAV default and legal default. Weekend eligibility maps to the next supplied common CLOSE, without extra grace. Terminal positions may remain unresolved; pending postterminal claims survive and later HOLD checkpoints may settle them without new investment.

Ordinary reductions precede additions. A short addition reserves a successful scenario locate for the entire frozen incremental quantity before either new side. Token account/path/formation/issuer/side/execution-event must match. It expires at event end, is never carried to another order/event, and is consumed only by actual added short fills. Actual borrow quantity persists until actual covers. Added short executes first; added long whole units are bounded by frozen target and actual newly shorted settled notional. Pure bounded admission search can only reduce requested quantities at fixed positive marks/prices/fees; no preview installs state or latches default.

Actual E<=0 is sticky DEFAULT_EVER and bars all later new risk, even after recovery. A rejected hypothetical negative poststate never latches actual default. Reconciled nondefault breach bars entry while flatten/blockers remain; only actual closure plus a later genuine formation can resume risk. Historical failed survival/overdue evidence persists. Unknown fills, residuals, unsupported physical crossing and unknown submit effects remain technical halts. Exact native fill/position identity and cent reconciliation remain authoritative; stock f64 Portfolio is not an exact oracle and native cash is not core free cash.

Saved verification regenerates no native fills or policy: it consumes the saved actual callbacks and declared event sequence, checks them through accepted pure core/trading arithmetic and the finite event algebra, and compares all records and final state. Native account/engine imports are unnecessary. It does not re-read quote/action/rate sources or write. Immutable store wrappers reuse existing canonical/hash/member checking and locked publication, without begin/attempt/budget mutations. They publish two PRIVATE members under signed-event-components; source-absent verification is read-only. Technical partial envelopes are retained with explicit unfinalized status and are never promoted to completed replay. Full source-aware producer, D sizing evidence, scheduler/runner/attempt authority, full study metrics and applicable retained/financial qualification remain separate.
